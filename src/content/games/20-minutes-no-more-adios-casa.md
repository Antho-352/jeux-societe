## About 20 minutes no more

Adios Casa is a one-person studio in Montpellier run by Arnaud Cebollada. Its escape games pair a short printable kit with a free app in which you chat with a virtual accomplice, a chatbot who is "inside" the scene and needs your help. 20 minutes no more is the free introduction: your partner is already in the Goldbank, the guard's next round is in 20 minutes, and you must solve the puzzles he sends you to open the vault door. The game is in French.

The product page lists it for 1 or 2 players, 20 to 30 minutes, beginner to intermediate, and notes a mechanism that lets you finish after the 20 minutes if you fail. Customer reviews from 2019 onwards describe it as a tutorial that makes you want the longer scenarios, which cost about 17 €. It is free, but it goes through the shop: add it to the cart at 0 € and the PDF unlocks after checkout.

## How to play in one minute

1. Order the free scenario on the site, download the PDF and print it (two copies if you are two).
2. Install the free Adios Casa app and start the scenario; your accomplice begins chatting.
3. Read the printed documents and the photos he sends, and answer his questions in the chat.
4. Type the codes you find; the app reacts, unlocks the next step or gives a hint.
5. Open the vault before the guard's round; a "Live" link lets a second player follow remotely.

## What's in the PDF

- A short kit, a few pages, with the documents your accomplice refers to; nothing to hide around the house.
- Prints in black and white or colour.
- In French; the chatbot speaks French too.
- Free 0 € checkout on the site required; the free app runs the game.
