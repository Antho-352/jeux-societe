## About the Chasse aux œufs de Pâques by Escape Kit

Escape Kit is a Paris publisher of paid printable escape games and treasure hunts, and its long blog post on free treasure hunts hides one genuinely free kit: a Chasse au trésor de Pâques for ages 7 to 9. The kit is in French. The idea is that the Easter bells have rung and the child must crack a short trail of puzzles to learn where the chocolate eggs are hidden.

The trail has four steps. Two messages are written in a simple letter-shift code and decode to the location of the next clue (the example reads "premier indice sous le canapé"). A rebus gives a third hiding place. At each stop the child also collects a keyword, and the three keywords together point to the eggs. A second file in the same Drive folder is a blank version you can personalise with your own hiding places.

## How to play in one minute

1. Open the Google Drive link in the post ("Chasse aux œufs"), print the one-page kit and hide the eggs.
2. Place coded message 2 and the first keyword where message 1 points, and so on down the trail.
3. Hand the child message 1 and a pen; they decode it letter by letter using the key.
4. The rebus (answer: réfrigérateur in the sample) leads to the last keyword.
5. With the three keywords, the child works out where the eggs are and the hunt ends in chocolate.

## What's in the PDF

- One A4 page in colour with the organiser's four steps, sample messages and solutions.
- A separate "énigme à personnaliser" PDF in the Drive folder for your own clues.
- Written in French; a non-reader will need help with the cipher.
- No signup: the Drive links are open.
