## About Bandido Covid-19 edition

Bandido is a cooperative card game by Martin Nedergaard Andersen in Helvetiq's Pocket Games collection. In the original, a bandit tries to tunnel out of jail. In this free print-and-play edition, made during the Covid-19 lockdowns, the escapee is a virus, and the cards also show everyday prevention gestures such as hand-washing and keeping your distance.

The rules are the same as the boxed game, so it is a free way to learn Bandido. It is a good first cooperative game for children from age 6, and it also plays solo. Helvetiq suggests letting younger players help with the cutting.

## How to play in one minute

1. Put the starting card with the virus in the middle of the table, shuffle the rest into a draw pile and deal 3 cards to each player.
2. The youngest player starts. On your turn, place one card so it fits perfectly against one or more cards already on the table, then draw a new card.
3. If you cannot play, put your cards under the draw pile and take three new ones.
4. Talk about the best move, but do not show your cards to the others.
5. You win together if every tunnel is blocked. If a tunnel is still open when the cards run out, the virus escapes and you all lose.
