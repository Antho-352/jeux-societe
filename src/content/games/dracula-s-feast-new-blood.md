## About Dracula's Feast: New Blood

Dracula's Feast: New Blood is the second edition of Dracula’s Feast and a logical deduction game for 4–8 players. Dracula’s dinner is interrupted by a mash of monsters, each with a secret identity.

The goal is to learn who everyone is through dances and clues, then accuse correctly. Jellybean Games identifies Peter C. Hayward and Tom Lang as designers and links the New Blood rulebook and card file on its print-and-play page.

## How to play in one minute

1. Prepare the guest and accusation cards.
2. Dance with other guests to exchange information.
3. Use the clues to narrow down each hidden identity.
4. Accuse once you can identify the guests.
