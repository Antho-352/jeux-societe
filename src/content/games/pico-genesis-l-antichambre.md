## About Pico Genesis

L'Antichambre runs twelve escape rooms in Paris, and its game masters wrote Pico Genesis as a free prequel to their Galaxie room. The game is in French. Doctor Nyme, a bio-informatics researcher, lost his wife and watches his daughter Leila talk to an imaginary friend called Pico; he decides to bring Pico to life, and creates a new generation of artificial intelligence, for better or worse. You work through his encrypted files to uncover that story.

It is rated medium difficulty, 1 to 5 players, 60 minutes. The 14-page booklet is the whole kit: you write on it, cut it and fold it. Every puzzle ends in a code of two to six digits that you type on the web page to unlock a video, and the videos hold clues for the next puzzle, so watch each one to the end. QR codes after each puzzle give three levels of hints. Searching the web is explicitly part of the game, so keep a phone or laptop handy.

## How to play in one minute

1. Download the booklet PDF, print it in colour (one copy for the team) and install a QR reader.
2. Play the introduction video on the page to set the scene.
3. Solve the puzzles in order, writing on and cutting the booklet as needed; research online when the puzzle asks.
4. Enter each puzzle's code on the page; the right code plays a video with the next clue.
5. Reach the end of the files in 60 minutes to learn how Pico was born.

## What's in the PDF

- 14 A4 landscape pages: the story, the puzzles, and QR codes for hints and solutions.
- Colour printing is needed; some puzzles rely on colour.
- Videos and code checks live on the web page, so the game is tied to an internet connection.
