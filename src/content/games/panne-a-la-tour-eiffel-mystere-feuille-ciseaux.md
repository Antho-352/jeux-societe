## About Panne à la Tour Eiffel

Mystère Feuille Ciseaux is a French site run by Etienne and Jeanne, two parents and long-time volunteer scout leaders who sell printable escape games for 3 to 11 year olds. Panne à la Tour Eiffel is their free sample for 9 to 11 year olds, and it is in French. The players have been invited to the "Fashion-sweet" gala at the top of the Eiffel Tower when the lift breaks down, sabotaged by the villain Mister G. They have 20 minutes to solve the puzzles and open the escape hatch before the sweets melt.

It is deliberately short and intense, built for a birthday afternoon or a rainy day, with an adult acting as game master. The puzzles use the mechanics the site recommends for this age: symbol ciphers, cross-referenced clues and paper elements to manipulate, including cut-out tools (a screwdriver, wires, a key) that unlock steps. Diplomas are included for the end. The PDF is free in exchange for an email address; the site says the address is only used for its monthly newsletter.

## How to play in one minute

1. Sign up on the page, print the kit and read the game-master sheet.
2. Cut out the puzzles, the tool cards and the clues, and hide or lay them out as instructed.
3. Read the story, start a 20-minute timer and let the children loose.
4. Each puzzle yields a code or a tool that unlocks the next step; the adult hands out hints when needed.
5. Open the hatch before the timer ends, then hand out the diplomas.
