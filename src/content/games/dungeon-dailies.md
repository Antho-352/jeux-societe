## About Dungeon Dailies

Dungeon Dailies is a print-and-play roll-and-write dungeon crawler by Shenbot Makes, meant to be short enough to play every day. It combines the item-and-monster combat of the designer's Morning Coffee Dungeon with the random mazes of The Crumbling Dungeon. Each map is a maze full of keys, chests, doors, stairs and monsters.

It is free to download on itch.io (name your own price) and suits solo players who like a quick puzzle with their coffee. You only need a pen and a set of polyhedral dice. Start with the demo map, which has the rules printed on it, then move on to the five numbered maps.

## How to play in one minute

1. Print a map and enter the dungeon at the entrance stairs.
2. Travel by drawing a line through the corridors until you reach a key, a treasure chest, a doorway, stairs or a monster.
3. Resolve the encounter with the dice: fight monsters, open chests for items, and use keys to get through doors.
4. Take the stairs to the next map and keep your valuables, and your head, intact.
5. Win by crossing every map and leaving through the final exit stairs.
