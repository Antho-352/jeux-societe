## About The Closest Ghost

The Closest Ghost is a two-player game by Andrew Looney, illustrated by Trin Hess, and the Looney Labs Holiday Gift for 2019. You and a friend are exploring a haunted house on Halloween when a ghost pops up and you both drop your trick-or-treat bags. The first to pick their candy back up wins, and the candy on the board is real.

Andrew Looney made it after a design challenge from candy-maker Greg Cohen of Lofty Pursuits. It was designed for Pyramid Arcade, but the household version needs only tokens, bottle-cap rings and sweets. It makes a good Halloween game for a parent and child, or two friends.

## How to play in one minute

1. Put the two boards side by side. Place the avatars on the two centre squares and a piece of candy on every other square.
2. Put two ghosts on the squares with corner dots. Whoever most recently offered candy goes first; the other player places the third ghost.
3. On your turn, move your avatar one space in a direction shown by an arrow (arrows off the edge wrap around). Pick up any candy you land on.
4. Then move the ghost closest to your avatar one space in any direction. Ghosts and avatars cannot share a space.
5. The first player to clear all the candy from their starting board wins.
