## About this printable Fox and Geese board

Fox and Geese is one of the oldest asymmetric board games in Europe, played on the same cross-shaped board as peg solitaire. This Fox and Geese game board printable from PrintablePaper.net (Savetz Publishing) gives you that board on one Letter page, with a red fox, thirteen white geese and the rules on the second page. It prints cleanly in black and white.

The two players have different jobs. The fox captures geese by jumping over them, like in checkers, and can chain jumps. The geese cannot jump at all; they win by crowding the fox until it has no legal move. It is a quick, thinky game that children pick up in minutes and that rewards the geese player who moves as a flock.

## How to use it

1. Print both pages and cut out the fourteen pieces, or use one coin and thirteen buttons.
2. Place the geese on the thirteen points of one arm and the adjacent row, and the fox in the centre (or anywhere, for variety).
3. The geese move first. Pieces move one step along a line to an empty point; only the fox jumps, and only over a single goose to an empty point beyond.
4. The geese win by trapping the fox; the fox wins by eating enough geese that they can no longer trap it.
