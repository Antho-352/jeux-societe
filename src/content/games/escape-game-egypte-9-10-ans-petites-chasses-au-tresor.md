## About the Escape Game Égypte 9-10 ans

Petites Chasses au Trésor, a French site run by Coline and Rémy since 2009, publishes "pedagogical adventures" for children, and this ancient Egypt escape game is one of its free kits. The kit is in French. The post offers three scenarios to read aloud (a 1922 dig racing to find a tomb, a cursed treasure, a sabotaged boat on the Nile) and a set of three puzzles that fit any of them.

Puzzle 1 is a word grid where the two hidden words (SABLE and TRÉSOR) trace a symbol pointing to the next envelope. Puzzle 2 is a message in hieroglyphs decoded with the alphabet printed on the sheet (answer: PHARAON). Puzzle 3 is a pyramid of additions whose top block gives the number 24. Solutions are on the page, so the adult can help. The only preparation is folding the five envelopes, and the hiding places are already chosen for you. To get the PDF you give an email address and the site sends its free kit library.

## How to play in one minute

1. Request the kits by email, print the Egypt pages and fold the five envelopes.
2. Hide them as instructed: puzzle 2 under the dining table, puzzle 3 under the child's favourite game, the answer envelope in a glass.
3. Read the scenario aloud and hand over the first envelope; start a 25-minute timer.
4. Each solved puzzle names the hiding place of the next envelope.
5. Opening the answer envelope in time confirms the mission is a success.

## What's in the PDF

- The scenario sheet, three puzzle sheets, and an answer envelope, all printed as foldable envelopes.
- Colour print recommended; the hieroglyph alphabet is on the same page as the message.
- Hiding spots and solutions are on the web page, not in the kit.
- Email signup required; the download is bundled with the site's other free kits.
