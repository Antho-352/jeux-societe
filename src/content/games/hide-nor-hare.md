## About Hide nor Hare

Hide nor Hare is a solo dice puzzle by D. Teuber. Hattie Hare's friends are playing hide and seek, and you place them, the trees they hide behind and the pigeons that watch them on a 6x6 grid. The game is based on Rabbits, a logic puzzle by Andrea Sabbatini.

It won Best PnP at the BoardGameGeek 2022 Solitaire Print and Play Contest and came second among dice-based games. The low-ink sheet turns it into a roll and write. It is a good pick for sudoku fans: every row and column must end with exactly one friend and one tree, and each pigeon must show the number of friends it can see in a straight line, with trees blocking the view.

## How to play in one minute

1. Sort the 40 tiles by type next to the board and keep your three flower tiles.
2. Each turn roll three dice; each die stands for a row or column of its number.
3. Place one friend, one tree and one pigeon (a 0, 1 or 2) in the row or column of a die each, never doubling a friend, tree or pigeon type in a line. If you cannot place a friend or tree, place a pigeon; if you cannot place a pigeon, cover one with a stone.
4. Spend a flower to reroll, clear stones or move a tile.
5. You win when 6 friends, 6 trees and 10 pigeons are placed and every pigeon's count is right. Score 3 points per pigeon and 1 per unused flower.
