## About Deep Space D-6

Deep Space D-6 is a solo dice game by Tony Go. You captain a small starship on patrol that answers a distress call and warps straight into a trap. With the help of your crew you must survive until a rescue fleet appears. It started as an entry in the 2015 BoardGameGeek Solitaire Print and Play Contest, was nominated for the 2015 Golden Geek award for Best Print & Play Board Game, and later became a boxed game from Tau Leader Games.

The original print-and-play files are still free on BoardGameGeek, uploaded by Tony Go himself (you need a free BGG account to download). His note on the file says this is the contest version, not a copy of the retail game, which adds fixes, more threats and three more ships. It is a quick, tense dice puzzle for solo players aged 10 and up and plays in about 30 minutes.

## How to play in one minute

1. Set out the ship board, shuffle the threat cards and put your crew dice next to the ship.
2. Each turn, roll your available crew dice. Each face is a crew role, and some results lock dice up or send them to the infirmary.
3. Assign crew to stations to act: fire on enemy ships, repair the hull, recharge shields, treat the wounded and deal with threats on board.
4. New threats arrive from the deck and activate on die rolls, damaging your hull or tying up your crew.
5. Survive the whole threat deck to win. If your hull is destroyed, or your whole crew is out of action, you lose.
