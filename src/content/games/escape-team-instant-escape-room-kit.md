## About the Escape Team Instant Escape Room Kit

This is the free starter kit Escape Team publishes for people who want a personalised escape room at a birthday, an office party or a chemistry class without designing one from scratch. It contains five puzzles that already work as a mission: a cut-out puzzle, a quiz, a dialling puzzle, a secret alphabet and a short scavenger hunt. Each puzzle ends in a five-digit code.

What makes it playable rather than just a template is the pairing with the Escape Team Mission Editor, a free web tool. You enter the five solution codes (printed on the kit page), set a countdown, add up to seven hints per puzzle and, if you like, your own MP3 narration. The editor returns an Escape Code; players type it into the free app and the mission runs itself. Sarah and Fabian, the couple behind Escape Team, suggest 2 to 4 players per device.

## How to play in one minute

1. Print the 7 pages, cut out the puzzle pieces and hide them in the locations the cheat sheet suggests (envelopes make it easier).
2. In the Mission Editor, enter the five codes from the kit page, a duration and some hints, and save to get an Escape Code.
3. Hand players a phone with the free app and the Escape Code loaded.
4. They solve the phases in order and type each code into the app.
5. The "mission success" text appears when all five are solved in time; put the location of a treasure chest in it for a prize.

## What's in the PDF

- 7 A4 pages: a pre-filled game-master cheat sheet, a blank cheat sheet, and the five puzzle pages with preparation notes.
- Prints in black and white; one printout per team.
- The puzzles are editable in spirit: swap the quiz questions or the alphabet for your own.
- Requires the free app on a phone or tablet and the free Mission Editor on the web.
