## About The Nordic Necklace

Clara Ridley, founder of the Amazing Adventure Society, receives a postcard from an old friend, and her next adventure begins. You follow the story page by page; every puzzle hides an answer of one or more words, and each answer unlocks the next part of the mystery of the Nordic Necklace.

Printable Escapes, the print-at-home escape room label of The Other Tales, offers this game as a free sample of its full-length adventures (Gatekeeper, Pirate's Quest). It is set up exactly like the paid games, only shorter: about 15 to 30 minutes. The publisher describes it as designed for adults but family friendly and a good one to try with teens. It suits 2 to 4 players best, and one person can solve it alone. With a bigger group, print two copies and race.

**How to get it:** the "Purchase now" button opens the publisher's DPD checkout, where the item is listed as "The Nordic Necklace - Free Game" at no cost. The cart first offers Gatekeeper at a discount; click "No thanks" to continue with the free game only.

## How to play in one minute

1. Check out the free game, download the PDF and print it on Letter paper. You can also play from a screen, but marking up the pages is more fun.
2. Start on the first game page; the story unfolds as you solve.
3. Solve each puzzle in turn; every answer is a word or a few words.
4. Stuck? The PDF includes hints, then the answers.
5. You win when the last answer reveals the secret of the necklace.

## What's in the PDF

- One 9-page PDF: 4 game pages, how-to-play instructions, hints and answers. Print everything or just the game pages.
- US Letter size; a printing service works too.
- No locks, app or props needed, only pens.
- To play over a video call, each household prints its own copy and you solve together.
