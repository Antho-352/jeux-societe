## About this printable Three Men's Morris board

This printable Three Men's Morris board from PrintablePaper.net (Savetz Publishing) gives you the board, 3 black and 3 white pieces to cut out, and the rules, on two black-and-white Letter pages. It is one of four morris boards from the same publisher; the best known is [Nine Men's Morris](/board-games/printable-nine-mens-morris/).

Three Men's Morris is the oldest and smallest of the family: a 3x3 grid of points and three pieces each, like tic-tac-toe with movement. All the morris games share one idea: get three pieces in a line, called a mill. Our [Nine Men's Morris rules page](/rules/nine-mens-morris/) explains the family in detail.

## How to use it

1. Cut out the pieces, or use coins and buttons in two colours.
2. Start with an empty board and decide who goes first.
3. Take turns placing one piece on any empty point.
4. Once all six pieces are down, slide one piece to an adjacent empty point along a line.
5. Three in a row wins, whether made while placing or moving.
