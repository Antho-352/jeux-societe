## About Ninjitsu!

Ninjitsu! is a card game for 2–5 players and a stand-alone sequel to Scuttle!. Players scheme to reach 21 points by playing cards or drawing two, using cards as treasure or actions.

Its defining twist is hidden treasure: cards can be played face down, then swiped by opponents—unless a booby trap is waiting. The official game page credits Peter C. Hayward for mechanics and Kelly Jo for art.

## How to play in one minute

1. Deal cards and make a draw deck.
2. On your turn, play one card or draw two cards.
3. Play cards as treasure or actions, including hidden treasures.
4. Use bluffs and traps while racing to 21 points.
