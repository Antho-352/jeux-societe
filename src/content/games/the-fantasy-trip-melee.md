## About Melee

Melee was Steve Jackson's second published game design and the combat core of The Fantasy Trip roleplaying game. Each counter is a single warrior: you build a fighter in a few minutes, pick weapons and armour, and send them into the arena. Winners get stronger and faster for the next fight; losers die. Steve Jackson Games now gives the PDF away at no charge on its Warehouse 23 store (a 2022 announcement also mentions DriveThruRPG).

It is a good introduction to tactical miniatures-style games for two players or more ("2+" on the publisher page) from age 10, and a natural first step into The Fantasy Trip.

## How to play in one minute

1. Create a fighter by splitting points between strength (ST) and dexterity (DX), then buy weapons and armour that your strength allows.
2. Place the counters on the hex arena map, facing the way you choose.
3. Each turn, roll for initiative, then move. A figure that ends next to an enemy is engaged and moves less freely.
4. To attack, roll three dice: equal to or below your adjusted DX is a hit. Roll the weapon's damage and subtract armour; the rest comes off the target's ST.
5. A figure at 0 ST is out. The last side standing wins, and survivors gain experience for the next fight.
