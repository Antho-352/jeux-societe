## About the Christmas Scavenger Hunt Clue Cards

This is the Christmas edition of Kimberly McLeod's clue-card hunts for The Best Ideas for Kids. Twelve numbered cards, each a short rhyme about Santa, cookies and sleighs, send children to everyday indoor spots: where you keep spoons, where the flour and sugar live, under a couch pillow, in the fridge, under a chair. The final card points to wherever most of the toys are kept, which is where you hide the big surprise.

It is written for any age: the wording ("look below", "close to") lets you make hiding spots easy for a four-year-old or sneaky for a ten-year-old. The family that designed it placed a small candy or non-candy treat at each clue, but one prize at the end works too. The PDF is a direct download in the post, with no email signup.

## How to play in one minute

1. Print the cards and cut them out; read the directions page.
2. Hide card 2 with the spoons, card 3 with the flour and sugar, and continue so each card sits where the previous clue points.
3. Hide the prize where the children's toys are kept, away from the rest of the trail.
4. Hand over card 1 and let them read, run and search.
5. The hunt ends at the prize; add your own extra cards before the last clue to stretch it.
