## About this printable Heads, Bodies and Legs sheet

Heads, Bodies and Legs is the drawing game the Surrealists called exquisite corpse: one player draws a head, folds the paper so only the neck lines show, and passes it on; the next draws a body, the next the legs, and the unfolded creature is revealed at the end. This printable from PrintablePaper.net (Savetz Publishing) makes it tidy, with a drawing sheet divided into three boxes and a cover sheet whose flaps hide each section as you go. The third page explains the rules and a caption variation.

It needs no drawing skill at all; the worse the drawings, the funnier the result. It works for two players and for a whole table, and it is a good quiet game for restaurants and waiting rooms.

## How to use it

1. Print the drawing sheet and the cover sheet. Cut the cover sheet along the dotted lines and fold back the flaps.
2. Lay the cover over the drawing sheet. The first player lifts the top flap, draws a head and closes it.
3. The next players draw a body, then legs, each seeing only their own box.
4. Lift the cover, admire the monster, and add a caption if you play the variation.
