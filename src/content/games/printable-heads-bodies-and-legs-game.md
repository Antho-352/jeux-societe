## About this printable Heads, Bodies and Legs sheet

Heads, Bodies and Legs is the fold-and-pass drawing game the Surrealists called exquisite corpse. One player draws a head, the next a body, the next the legs, each without seeing the others, and the creature is revealed at the end. This printable from PrintablePaper.net (Savetz Publishing) makes it tidy: a drawing sheet with three boxes, a cover sheet whose flaps hide each section, and a page of rules.

It needs no drawing skill; the worse the drawings, the funnier the result. It is a good quiet game for restaurants and waiting rooms.

## How to use it

1. Print both sheets. Cut the cover sheet along the dotted lines and fold back the flaps.
2. Lay the cover on the drawing sheet. The first player lifts the top flap, draws a head and closes it.
3. The next players draw a body, then legs, each seeing only their own box.
4. Lift the cover and enjoy the monster; add a caption if you like.
