## About Jabberwocky

Jabberwocky is a collection of eight games built around one setting and card system. The official page lists Bandersnatch, Borogoves, Brillig, Gimble, Gyre, Mimsy, Mome, and Slithy, covering solo, two-player, and group play.

Its games range from a solo puzzle and map-making to bidding, rummy, area control, mancala, bridge building, and negotiation. The print-and-play page links a rulebook plus rule and number-card files.

## How to play in one minute

1. Choose one of the eight included games and read its setup.
2. Prepare the shared card material required by that game.
3. Follow its specific turn structure and objective.
4. Use the rulebook when switching to another Jabberwocky game.
