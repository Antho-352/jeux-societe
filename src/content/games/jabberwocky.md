## About Jabberwocky

A single deck of number cards that plays five different games: Bandersnatch (solo), Borogoves (1-2 player map-making), Gyre (2-3 player area control), Mimsy (2-3 player mancala) and Slithy (3-7 player negotiation). Themed on Lewis Carroll's poem with art by Tania Walker.
