## About Dracula's Feast

Dracula's Feast is a logical deduction game for 4–8 players. At Dracula’s dinner, monsters have mixed with the townsfolk, and each guest has a unique hidden identity to discover.

Players dance with other guests, gather information, and accuse once they think they have solved the table. The publisher lists this original Unmasked-series edition alongside its free print-and-play cards and English rules.

## How to play in one minute

1. Set out the guest and accusation cards.
2. Meet and dance with other guests to learn clues about identities.
3. Track what each clue rules in or out.
4. When you have solved the guests, make accusations to win.
