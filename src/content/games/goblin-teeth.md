## About Goblin Teeth

A dice-bidding game for 2-5 goblins. Each turn you place a die on an item or sacrifice it on the altar for sneaky Cheat cards; dice on items may not tie and may not sum above ten. Collect the set of shiny bits Goblin Big Boss wants to win.
