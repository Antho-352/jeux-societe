## About Goblin Teeth

Goblin Teeth is a dice-bidding game for 2–5 players. Goblins roll dice, compete for items, and try to be the first to assemble the offering Goblin Big Boss wants.

On a turn, a die can go on an item or be sacrificed at the altar for a Cheat card. The official description adds two placement limits: players may not tie on an item, and their dice there cannot total more than ten.

## How to play in one minute

1. Roll your dice and assess the available items.
2. Place a die on an item or sacrifice it at the altar.
3. Use Cheat cards and obey the no-tie and total-of-ten limits.
4. Claim loot at round end and complete the Big Boss offering to win.
