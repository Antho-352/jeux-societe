## About this printable Twelve Men's Morris board

This printable Twelve Men's Morris board from PrintablePaper.net (Savetz Publishing) gives you the board, 12 black and 12 white pieces to cut out, and the rules, on two black-and-white Letter pages. It is part of a set of four morris boards from the same publisher; the best known is [Nine Men's Morris](/board-games/printable-nine-mens-morris/).

Twelve Men's Morris adds diagonal lines to the Nine Men's Morris board and gives each player twelve pieces, so all 24 points can be filled. In southern Africa it is played as Morabaraba, with tournaments and a national federation. Diagonals mean mills can be made in three directions, and if all pieces are placed without a capture the game is a draw. The morris games are among the oldest board games in Europe, and all of them share the same idea: get three of your pieces in a line, called a mill. Our [Nine Men's Morris rules page](/rules/nine-mens-morris/) explains the family in more detail.

## How to use it

1. Print both pages and cut out the pieces, or use coins and buttons in two colours.
2. Start with an empty board and decide who goes first.
3. Take turns placing one piece on any empty point.
4. If the board fills without any capture, the game is a draw. Otherwise slide one piece per turn to an adjacent empty point; a player down to three pieces may fly to any empty point.
5. Every new mill, along a row, column or diagonal, removes an opponent's piece. A player reduced to two pieces loses.
