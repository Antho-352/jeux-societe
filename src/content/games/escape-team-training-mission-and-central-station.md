## About Escape Team: Training Mission and Central Station

Escape Team is a printable escape room made by Sarah, an educator, and Fabian, a UX design professor. You print a few pages, open the free app on a phone, and the app tells the story through audio while a countdown runs. Every mission is split into phases, each ending in a five-digit code that you type into the app; a wrong code costs time.

The first PDF bundles the Training Mission (maximum 10 minutes, rated easy) with Mission 1, Central Station (maximum 15 minutes), where you work as a team to stop a bomb. Both are free, including their app content; from Mission 2 onwards the PDFs are free but the app levels cost $2.99 each. The puzzles are drawn, cut, folded and written on, which is why the site insists on a real printout rather than a screen.

## How to play in one minute

1. Print the 12 pages single-sided, one page per sheet, and put scissors, coloured pens and tape on the table.
2. Install the free Escape Team app, turn the sound on and choose Training Mission.
3. Spread all the mission pages out and work on them in parallel; each phase's overview lists what it needs.
4. Solve a phase, enter its five-digit code in the app, and move to the next one.
5. Enter the final code before the countdown ends. Then run Central Station the same way.

## What's in the PDF

- 12 A4 pages: a how-to page, the five-page Training Mission with three phases, and the six-page Central Station mission.
- Cut-out membership cards as props and a bonus offer: post a selfie of your group and the team sends a coupon for missions 2 to 4 (Android only).
- Prints fine in black and white; coloured pens are for you, not the printer.
- A phone or tablet with the free app is required; one device per group of four.
