## About the Escape Game Halloween 7-9 ans

This is the Halloween kit from Petites Chasses au Trésor, the French children's treasure-hunt site run by Coline and Rémy. The kit is in French. The post gives three ready-made stories: a mummy who curses you until you return her three amulets before midnight, an army of the undead, and a three-fingered witch demanding her three rings. In every version the children have 25 minutes.

Three puzzles are printed as envelopes to hide. Puzzle 1 is a letter grid in which the words FANTÔME and TOMBEAU, once highlighted, draw a symbol pointing to the next envelope. Puzzle 2 is a message in spooky symbols; the key is hidden on the envelope itself and the answer is "sarcophage". Puzzle 3 is a pyramid of additions whose top block is 32. The author sizes it for 7 to 9 year olds in 25 to 30 minutes, longer for 5 and 6 year olds with help, shorter for 10 and up. A cellar, garage or attic makes the ideal venue. The PDF is free but arrives by email after you join the site's list.

## How to play in one minute

1. Request the free kits, print the Halloween pages and fold the envelopes.
2. Hide them in the spots listed on the page, ideally somewhere dusty with real cobwebs.
3. Read one of the three scenarios aloud, hand over the first envelope and start the timer.
4. Each solved puzzle reveals where the next envelope is hidden.
5. The answer envelope, found last, tells the children whether they broke the curse.

## What's in the PDF

- Scenario, three puzzle sheets and an answer sheet, designed to fold into envelopes.
- Colour print recommended for the Halloween artwork.
- Solutions and hiding places are on the web page for the adult.
- Email signup required; the kit comes with the site's other free kits.
