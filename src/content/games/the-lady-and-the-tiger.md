## About The Lady and the Tiger

The Lady and the Tiger packages five games into eighteen unique cards. The official page lists Doors, Favor, Hoard, Labyrinth, and Traps, each with its own player count and style of play.

The collection is inspired by Frank R. Stockton’s short story. Its print-and-play catalogue links the individual English PDFs, a full set through the publisher’s shared folder, and translated Italian rules.

## How to play in one minute

1. Choose one of the five games.
2. Prepare the cards and any tokens that game requires.
3. Follow its own objective and turn rules.
4. Use the relevant rules document when changing games.
