## About The Lady and the Tiger

Eighteen cards that play five different games: Doors (2-player bluffing), Favor (2-4 player bidding), Hoard (solo), Labyrinth (2-player maze) and Traps (2-6 player bluffing). Inspired by Frank R. Stockton's short story.
