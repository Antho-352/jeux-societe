## About the Bible Escape Room

This DIY escape room by Mary of My Party Games is built around the miracles performed by Jesus. A backstory card sets the scene, seven clues and puzzles follow, and the last one opens a final message. Every puzzle revolves around the miracle stories, so it also works as a lively review after a lesson.

The author made it for all age groups and suggests playing it at home, at church, in a Bible study group or in a classroom. A team of 3 or 4 players works best; for more people, print two or three copies of the clues and run several teams. Everything is free for personal use, and the post includes a tips sheet and full answer keys for the leader.

## How to play in one minute

1. Print the backstory on A4 and the seven clue sheets, and prepare the clues that need cutting.
2. Hand the backstory to the players. Give them the other clues one by one, or hide them around the room for a harder game.
3. Clue 2 comes with a rhyme to decode; clue 3 is a cut-up sheet to put back in order; clue 4 is written in Braille across two hidden sheets.
4. Clue 5 needs two sheets, one of them cut into pieces with holes; clue 7 is printed once per team.
5. The team that solves all seven clues reads the final message. Share tips if a team is stuck.
