## About Master Udo Vs. the Four Martial Arts Masters

Master Udo is a solo fighting game by Jorge Zhang in which you prove you are the strongest martial artist around by beating four masters one after another. It finished 4th overall at the BoardGameGeek 2022 Solitaire Print and Play Contest.

The combo system is the star. Every card in your hand is split in two: the left half is a condition and the right half is an effect. You play cards on top of each other so only the last card's effect, the finisher, is visible, and most effects scale with X, the length of the combo. Your hand is also your health: unblocked attacks make you discard, and when the cards are gone you lose.

## How to play in one minute

1. Take the 10 starter cards into your hand, shuffle the attack deck, lay out the master cards and set the charge tracker to 0.
2. Pick a master. Each turn, reveal attack cards equal to the swords on that master; they show which body part is targeted.
3. Play any number of combos, checking every card's condition, then resolve each finisher: deal X damage to the master's hearts or block attacks.
4. Unblocked attacks force you to discard a named card from your hand, or exhaust one from your discard pile for good.
5. When all of a master's hearts reach 0, take your discards back, add that master's special move to your hand and challenge the next one. Beat the fourth master to win.
