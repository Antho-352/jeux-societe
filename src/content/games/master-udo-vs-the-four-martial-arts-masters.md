## About Master Udo Vs. the Four Martial Arts Masters

Best Overall #4. A 9-card solo fighter: each card is split into a combo restriction and a combo effect, so you chain cards into long combos to damage four successive martial-arts masters while your hand dwindles like in Gloomhaven.
