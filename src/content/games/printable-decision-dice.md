## About this printable decision die

The decision dice template from PrintablePaper.net (Savetz Publishing) is a one-page cube net whose six faces answer a question for you: YES, NO, MAYBE, ASK LATER, NO WAY and ROLL AGAIN. It does the job of a magic 8-ball without the water, and it is a quick craft for children who like making things that are immediately useful.

It is not a game on its own, but it is a handy tiebreaker for games: who goes first, whether to take the risky move, whether the house rule applies tonight. Pair it with the publisher's [chore die](/dice-games/printable-chore-dice/) for a full set of paper oracles.

## How to use it

1. Print on card stock, or on paper that you then glue to thin cardboard.
2. Cut around the outline, score and fold the lines, and glue the tabs inside the cube.
3. Ask your question out loud, roll, and accept the answer. ROLL AGAIN means exactly that.
