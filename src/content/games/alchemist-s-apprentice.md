## About Alchemist's Apprentice

Winner of Best Overall Game (and seven other categories). A solo dice-rolling puzzle with a narrative campaign: each turn you roll ingredient dice and fit them into a potion formula while managing volatility, with new ingredients and mechanics unlocked chapter by chapter.
