## Printable puzzles by age

- **Under 6:** easy mazes with wide paths, dot to dots of 10 to 17 dots, and word searches with short words that read left to right, such as colours and sight words.
- **Ages 6 to 8:** medium mazes, dot to dots of 18 to 40 dots, the alphabet dot to dots, 4 × 4 sudoku and most of the bingo sets.
- **Ages 9 to 13:** 6 × 6 sudoku, medium word searches with diagonal words, and harder mazes and dot to dots.
- **Teens and adults:** hard mazes, 20 × 20 word searches in all eight directions, dot to dots of up to 172 dots, and 9 × 9 sudoku books from easy to expert.

## Made to print

Every puzzle here is our own work, in black and white, sized for both A4 and US Letter. Print at 100 % scale with no page scaling, on plain paper. The answers are at the end of each PDF, so you can print only the puzzle pages and keep the solution.
