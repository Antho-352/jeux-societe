## What's in each bingo PDF

Each themed set has 20 unique 5 × 5 cards, two to a page and numbered, with a FREE square in the middle. The last page holds what the caller needs: 36 calling cards to cut out for the word bingos, or a call sheet from 1 to 75 for classic number bingo. Everything is black and white, for A4 or US Letter.

## Choosing a bingo

- **Holidays:** Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Valentine's Day and Easter bingo, each built from 36 seasonal words.
- **Parties:** baby shower and bridal shower bingo, written for adults.
- **Class and travel:** classroom bingo with school words, and road trip bingo for the car.
- **Numbers:** classic 75-ball bingo, with the usual columns from B 1–15 to O 61–75, for family nights, care homes or maths practice.
- **Blank templates:** a 5 × 5 card with a FREE square and a 3 × 3 mini card, to fill in by hand.

## How to call a word bingo

Cut out the calling cards, fold them and draw them from a hat or a bowl. Read each word aloud and set the card aside. When someone calls bingo, check their card against the pile of cards already drawn.
