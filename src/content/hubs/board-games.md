## Three kinds of board games

- **Print-and-play games:** complete games released as PDFs, such as Kill Doctor Lucky, the original 1996 Cheapass game, or Pandemic: Hot Zone – North America, a short cooperative Pandemic from Z-Man Games. Carcassonne has a free demo with land tiles to print.
- **Solo games:** Awaken the Ancients, Building Stonehenge, Garden Builder and Tubbataha are built for one player. Awaken the Ancients won the 2024 BoardGameGeek solitaire print-and-play contest.
- **Classic boards:** chess, checkers, go (9 × 9, 13 × 13 and 19 × 19), backgammon, mancala, nine men's morris, Chinese checkers, shogi and more, from PrintablePaper.net, for the games everyone already knows.

Two free expansions, Kingdomino: The Court and Small World: The Lost Tribe's Crusade, need the boxed game to play.
