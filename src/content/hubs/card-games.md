## Which card game to print?

- **Party games:** Meow!, a game of secret rules for 3 to 12 players inspired by Mao, and Dracula's Feast and Night of the Mummy, deduction games for 4 to 8 players where everyone hides an identity.
- **Quick family games:** Brains!, Scuttle! and Ninjitsu! each play in 5 to 10 minutes, from age 6.
- **Famous games to try:** free print-and-play files of Love Letter, Citadels, Spot It! (Dobble) and Timeline from Asmodee. The last three are demos of the boxed game.
- **Solo card games:** Orchard, a 10-minute game of 9 cards, Picklock!, played with a standard deck, Zombie in My Pocket and Mini Rogue, plus a solo mode for 7 Wonders Duel.
- **A long game:** Unfair, a theme-park building game for 2 to 5 players aged 14 and up (50 to 125 minutes), with two expansions and a solo mode.

## Before you print

Check the page count on each game: the files run from 7 pages for Ripples and Branches to 141 for Unfair. The page also says whether the cards are in colour and whether the download is hosted here or on the designer's site.
