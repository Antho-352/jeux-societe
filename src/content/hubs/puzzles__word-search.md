## Which word search for which age?

Our word searches come in three levels. The level sets both the size of the grid and the directions the words can run.

- **Easy (10 × 10 grid, 15 words):** words read left to right or top to bottom only. Made for children of about 4 to 8 who are learning to read, with themes like colours, sight words, the farm and the weather.
- **Medium (15 × 15 grid, 18 to 20 words):** words also run diagonally, but never backwards. Right for confident readers from about 6 to 13, and for adults who want a relaxed puzzle.
- **Hard (20 × 20 grid, 20 words):** words run in all eight directions, backwards included. This is where you'll find the hard word search for adults, the countries of the world and the science grid.

## Holiday and classroom word searches

There is a word search for each big date of the school year: back to school, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, winter, Valentine's Day, Easter and summer. Topic grids such as animals, the human body, space, math and music make a calm start or end to a lesson.

## Printing

Every PDF has two pages: the grid with its word list, then the answer key. Print page 1 for the players and keep page 2 for checking. They are black and white and print on A4 or US Letter at 100 % scale.
