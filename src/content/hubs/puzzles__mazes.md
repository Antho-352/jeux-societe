## Which maze for which age?

- **Easy (grids of 9 × 9 to 12 × 14):** wide paths and a single route. The dinosaur, farm, circle, pumpkin, heart and Easter egg mazes suit children from about 3; the rocket, star, fish and Christmas tree mazes suit ages 6 to 8.
- **Medium (20 × 20 to 24 × 26):** dead ends and narrower corridors for 6 to 12 year-olds. There is one for each season and holiday (Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, winter, spring, Easter, Valentine's Day, summer), plus a dragon and a space maze.
- **Hard (36 × 36 to 48 × 48):** thin walls, loops and several false exits. Expect ten minutes or more, even for adults.

## Mazes in every shape

The hard level is also where the shaped mazes are: circular, hexagon, triangle and diamond, plus a snowflake, a haunted house and a lost temple.

All our mazes print on a single A4 or Letter sheet in black and white, so a whole class can be printed in a minute. The solution is on page 2.
