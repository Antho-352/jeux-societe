## Which hunt for which day?

- **Outdoors:** the nature scavenger hunt has 16 things to find, such as a caterpillar, a spiderweb and an acorn. The beach hunt lists 20 things to spot on the shore, from a white shell to a buoy.
- **Indoors:** the indoor hunt sends children around the house for "something green" or "something that sparkles", which makes it a good rainy-day game.
- **Holidays:** a Halloween list of decorations to spot while walking the neighbourhood, a Christmas lights checklist for the car, and rhyming clue cards for Christmas and for Easter.

## Checklist or clue trail?

A checklist hunt is a list of things to find or tick off. There is nothing to prepare, and each child goes at their own pace.

A clue trail is a chain of hiding places. Each card is hidden where the previous clue points, and the last one leads to a prize. It takes a little setup before the children arrive, but it feels like a real treasure hunt.
