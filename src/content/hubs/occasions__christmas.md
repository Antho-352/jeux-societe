## Christmas printables by age

- **Under 6:** the star and Christmas tree dot to dots, with 10 and 15 dots.
- **From 6:** Christmas bingo, the Christmas word search, the easy Christmas tree maze and the Christmas maze, the alphabet gingerbread man (A to Z), and the gingerbread man and snowman dot to dots (34 and 40 dots).
- **Teens and adults:** the 141-dot reindeer and the snowflake maze, a 44 × 44 grid that takes a while even for adults.

## Christmas games for a group

Christmas bingo is the easiest game for a family gathering or a class party. The set has 20 unique cards built from 36 Christmas words, with calling cards to draw from a hat.

At home, the Christmas scavenger hunt clue cards are 12 rhyming clues that lead children from the spoon drawer to the couch, and finally to the toys, where you hide the surprise. In the car or on a walk, the Christmas light scavenger hunt is a checklist of things to spot.

The puzzles and bingo cards are our own, in black and white. The two scavenger hunts are by their creators, who offer them free on their own sites.
