## Thanksgiving printables by age

- **From 6:** Thanksgiving bingo, and the pumpkin and squirrel dot to dots (30 and 32 dots).
- **From 9:** the Thanksgiving word search and the Thanksgiving maze, a step harder.
- **Teens and adults:** the fox dot to dot, with 147 dots.

## A plan for the day

Before the meal, hand out the word search, the maze or a dot to dot to keep the kids' table busy while the turkey cooks.

Once everyone is seated, or after dessert, Thanksgiving bingo brings the whole family in. The set has 20 unique cards built from 36 Thanksgiving words, with calling cards to draw from a hat.

Everything on this page is our own work, in black and white, for A4 or US Letter. Each PDF ends with its answer key or, for bingo, the calling cards.
