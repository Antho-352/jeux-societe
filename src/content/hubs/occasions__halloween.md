## Halloween printables by age

- **Under 6:** the ghost and bat dot to dots (14 and 17 dots) and the easy pumpkin maze.
- **From 6:** Halloween bingo, the pumpkin dot to dot and the Halloween maze. The Pumpkin Patch Mystery is an escape room for a child aged 7 to 12 and a grown-up (15 to 20 minutes), and Brains!, a zombie card game for 2 to 5 players, starts at age 6.
- **From 8 to 13:** the Halloween word search, the Witch's Curse escape room (1 to 5 players, 45 minutes) and The Last Trick-or-Treat, a two-player escape room from age 10. In class, The Haunted Classroom suits grades 3 to 5.
- **Teens and adults:** the haunted house maze, the 116-dot barn owl, and the Halloween Escape Room for 13+ and adults.

## For a Halloween party

For 4 to 8 guests, Dracula's Feast and Night of the Mummy are short deduction games: everyone hides an identity and tries to unmask the others. Two players can share The Closest Ghost, a Looney Labs game played with real candy.

For the walk between houses, the Halloween scavenger hunt is a list of decorations to tick off, with a picture version for preschoolers.
