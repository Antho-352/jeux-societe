## Where to start

New to card games? Go Fish, War and Crazy Eights teach the basics to children from age 4 to 6. For a family evening, try Rummy or Spoons. If you want a partnership game with real strategy, Spades and Euchre are the American classics; Hearts and Whist are the British ones. Cribbage is the best two-player game in the list, and Klondike is the solitaire everyone knows.

Where sources disagree on a rule, each page says so and gives both versions, so you can agree on one before dealing.
