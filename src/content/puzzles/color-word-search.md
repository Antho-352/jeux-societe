## About this word search

Fifteen colour names in a 10 × 10 grid, from red and blue to silver and violet. It is designed for children aged about 4 to 7 who are learning to read, so every word reads left to right or top to bottom and most are short. Pair it with crayons: colouring each word in its own colour is half the activity.

## What's in the PDF

- Page 1: the 10 × 10 grid (words read left to right or top to bottom) and the list of 15 words to find.
- Page 2: the answer key, with every word highlighted.
- Black and white, US Letter with 18 mm margins, so it also prints on A4 at 100 % scale.

**Tip:** Start with the colours the child can already spell (RED, BLUE, PINK); the rest of the list is a chance to learn the others.
