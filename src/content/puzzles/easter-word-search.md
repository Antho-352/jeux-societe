## About this word search

Fifteen Easter words in a 10 × 10 grid: bunny, egg, basket, chick, lamb, tulip, jellybean. It is designed for children aged about 5 to 8, so the words read only left to right or top to bottom and none is longer than nine letters. Slip it into an Easter basket or use it while the eggs are being hidden.
