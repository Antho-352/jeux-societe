## About this dot to dot

A snowman in a top hat. The face, the buttons and the stick arms are printed; the dots trace the three snowballs and the hat. Add a scarf at the end.

## What's in the PDF

- Page 1: the puzzle, 40 numbered dots with the starting point circled.
- Page 2: the finished picture, to check the drawing or to use as a colouring page.
