## About this maze

A maze shaped like a six-petal flower, for children aged about 6 to 9. You enter at the top petal, pass through the centre and leave through the bottom petal; the four side petals are there to lead you astray. It suits a spring classroom pack, a garden topic or a Mother's Day card.
