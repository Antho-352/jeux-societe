## About these bingo cards

A nine-square bingo card for the youngest players and for very short games. Page 1 has one large card, page 2 has four small cards to cut apart. There is no FREE square: with only nine boxes, every square counts. Draw pictures or stick stickers in the squares for toddlers, or write words for a quick classroom warm-up.

## What's in the PDF

- Page 1: one large blank 3 × 3 card, with lines for the theme and a name.
- Page 2: 4 smaller blank 3 × 3 cards to cut apart.
- Black and white, US Letter with 18 mm margins, so it also prints on A4 at 100 % scale.

**Tip:** For pre-readers, use the same nine pictures on every card in a different order; the game then only needs a 'line' to be won.
