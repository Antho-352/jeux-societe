A medium-difficulty maze in the shape of a Christmas tree, for children aged about 6 to 9. The route starts at the star, winds through the three tiers of branches and ends at the trunk. It takes ten minutes or so and prints in black and white, so it goes well in a Christmas activity pack or on the kids' table at a family dinner.
