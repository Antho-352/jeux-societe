## About this dot to dot

An old sailing ship with three masts, square sails and a high stern, drawn in several lines. Long straight runs and tight corners, for adults who like a slow drawing.

## What's in the PDF

- Page 1: the puzzle, 143 numbered dots with the starting point circled.
- Page 2: the finished picture, to check the drawing or to use as a colouring page.
- Black and white, prints on A4 or US Letter at 100% scale.
