## About this sudoku book

One hundred hard sudoku puzzles, two to a page so there is room for pencil marks. Each grid has around 27 given numbers and a unique solution, and none can be finished with singles alone, so you will need pairs, pointing candidates and patience. It is a month or two of daily puzzles for an experienced solver, in one PDF.

- Pages 1–50: 100 9 × 9 puzzles, 2 per page (27 clues each), every one with a unique solution.

- Black and white, US Letter with 18 mm margins, so it also prints on A4 at 100 % scale.
