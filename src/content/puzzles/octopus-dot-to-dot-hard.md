## About this dot to dot

An octopus with eight curling arms. The curls pack the dots close together, which makes it the hardest picture of the set: take your time with the numbers.

## What's in the PDF

- Page 1: the puzzle, 90 numbered dots with the starting point circled.
- Page 2: the finished picture, to check the drawing or to use as a colouring page.
- Black and white, prints on A4 or US Letter at 100% scale.
