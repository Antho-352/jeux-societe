## About this maze

A hard maze in the shape of a snowflake, for adults and older teens. The six arms and their branches are packed with dead ends, and the only way from the top arm to the bottom arm passes through the central hexagon. It prints well on card for a Christmas table or a winter puzzle evening.

## What's in the PDF

- Page 1: the snowflake-shaped maze (44 × 44 grid, 864 cells), with Start and Finish marked by arrows.
- Page 2: the solution, a red path 256 cells long.
- Black and white, US Letter with 18 mm margins, so it also prints on A4 at 100 % scale.

**Tip:** Solve the centre hexagon first, then find which gap in the top arm reaches it and which gap in the bottom arm leaves it; the side arms can be ignored.
