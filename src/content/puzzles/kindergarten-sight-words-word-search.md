## About this word search

Fifteen kindergarten sight words in a 10 × 10 grid: the, and, you, said, like, look, can, see, play, come, here, little, big, run, jump. It is for children aged about 4 to 6 who are learning to recognise these words on sight. Everything reads left to right or top to bottom, like a book.

## What's in the PDF

- Page 1: the 10 × 10 grid (words read left to right or top to bottom) and the list of 15 words to find.
- Page 2: the answer key, with every word highlighted.
- Black and white, US Letter with 18 mm margins, so it also prints on A4 at 100 % scale.

**Tip:** Have the child read each word aloud from the list, find it, then read it again in the grid: three repetitions per word is the point of the exercise.
